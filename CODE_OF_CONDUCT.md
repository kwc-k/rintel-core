# Code of conduct

We want participation in Rintel Core to be respectful and useful. Be considerate, discuss evidence and technical decisions rather than attacking people, and make room for different levels of experience. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may restrict participation when needed. If you need to report conduct privately, request a private contact channel through the maintainer's GitHub profile without posting sensitive details. We will assess reports in context and avoid sharing them beyond those needed to address the issue.
